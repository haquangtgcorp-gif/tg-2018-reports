Rules for every report
  • Report date = previous working day (Monday reports Friday). Weekly = ClickUp week Mon to Sun.
  • Real numbers only. A missing source says No data. Actions are marked proposed.
  • Sources are read only: no WhatsApp sends, no Sheet or ClickUp edits.
  • File name 2018_<Report>_<date>.pdf. A delivered file is never overwritten.
  • Saved in 5. Reports/<folder>, C:\Quang Ha\T&G\PM\2018 and the brain PM folder.
Daily and weekly rhythm
  • Every day 9:00: 4 Progress reports and the Daily Staff and Task Report.
  • Every day: NWD Register into the Destec folder.
  • Monday 9:00: Weekly Manhours Report (last week) and Weekly Deadline Look-ahead (this week).
  • Friday after 17:00: Weekly Client Report, same day the NWD goes to the client.
  • Monthly: ClickUp monthly timesheet; KPI results once KPIs are agreed.
